# About ACME

ACME Ltd makes dependable widgets and gadgets for people who need things that
work. Founded in 2019, we are a remote-first company of about 120 people.

Our two product lines are [[widget-pro]] and [[gadget-lite]]. If you are new
here, start with [[onboarding]] and see [[office-locations]] for where we hub.

# Values

We keep three values short enough to remember: ship it properly, say the hard
thing kindly, and measure before you argue.
