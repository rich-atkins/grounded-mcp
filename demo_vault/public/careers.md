# Careers

We hire for kindness and craft, in that order. Open roles are listed on the
website; internal referrals carry a £1,000 bonus, paid after the new starter
passes probation (three months).

## Interview process

Four stages: screening call, take-home exercise (capped at three hours), a
pairing session, and a values conversation. We aim to complete the loop inside
two weeks.
