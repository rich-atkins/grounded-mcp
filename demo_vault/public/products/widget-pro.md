# Widget Pro

Widget Pro is ACME's flagship industrial widget, rated for continuous duty at
up to 4,000 RPM. It ships with a five-year warranty.

## Pricing

Widget Pro lists at £249 per unit, with volume discounts from 50 units.
Education customers get 15% off list price.

## Support

Support hours are 09:00–17:00 UK time, Monday to Friday. Enterprise customers
have a 4-hour response SLA. See [[gadget-lite]] for the consumer line.
