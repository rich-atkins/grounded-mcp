# Widget Pro (2025 datasheet)

Widget Pro is ACME's flagship industrial widget, rated for continuous duty at
up to 3,500 RPM. It ships with a three-year warranty.

## Pricing

Widget Pro lists at £199 per unit, with volume discounts from 100 units.
Education customers get 10% off list price.

## Support

Support hours are 09:00–17:00 UK time, Monday to Friday. Enterprise customers
have an 8-hour response SLA.
