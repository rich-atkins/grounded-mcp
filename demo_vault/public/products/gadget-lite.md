# Gadget Lite

Gadget Lite is the consumer companion to [[widget-pro]]: smaller, quieter, and
designed for desk use. It comes in three colours (slate, moss, and clay).

## Pricing

Gadget Lite retails at £79. There is no volume discount on the consumer line.

## Battery

The battery lasts about 14 hours of continuous use and charges over USB-C in
90 minutes.
