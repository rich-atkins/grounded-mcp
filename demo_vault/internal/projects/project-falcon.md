# Project Falcon

Falcon is the 2026 replatforming of the widget telemetry backend from the
legacy queue to event streaming.

## Status

As of August 2026 Falcon is in phase two of three: dual-writing to both
systems with parity checks running nightly. Cutover is targeted for October.

## Team

Falcon is led by the platform squad with two embedded engineers from the
widget team. Decisions are logged in the Falcon decision doc, not in chat.
