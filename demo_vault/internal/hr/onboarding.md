# Onboarding

Welcome aboard. Your first-week buddy is named in your welcome email.

## First week

Day one is kit and accounts. Days two to four are team shadowing. Friday is
your first ship — everyone merges something in week one, however small.

## Desk booking

Book hub desks in the facilities app; see [[office-locations]] for the hubs.
Probation is three months with a written check-in at six weeks.
