# Expenses Policy (2025)

Spend ACME's money like it is your own on a careful day.

## Limits

Meals while travelling: up to £35 per day. Hotel cap: £120 per night in the
UK, £140 elsewhere. Anything above the caps needs manager approval BEFORE
booking.

## Claims

Claim within 14 days by emailing receipts to the finance inbox. Mileage is
paid at 40p per mile.
