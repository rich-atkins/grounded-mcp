# Expenses Policy

Spend ACME's money like it is your own on a careful day.

## Limits

Meals while travelling: up to £40 per day. Hotel cap: £140 per night in the
UK, £160 elsewhere. Anything above the caps needs manager approval BEFORE
booking.

## Claims

Claim within 30 days through the expenses app, with receipts. Mileage is paid
at the standard HMRC rate.
