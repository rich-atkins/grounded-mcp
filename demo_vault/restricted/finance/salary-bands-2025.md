# Salary Bands 2025

Confidential — People team and executives only.

## Engineering

- Engineer I: £42,000–£52,000
- Engineer II: £52,000–£65,000
- Senior: £65,000–£80,000
- Staff: £80,000–£95,000

## Bonus

The company bonus pool for 2025 is 6% of salary at target, paid in April.
