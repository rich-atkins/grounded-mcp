# Project Nimbus

Codename for the possible acquisition of Cumulus Tools GmbH. Known to the
board and the deal team only.

## Status

Term sheet drafted in July 2026; due diligence starts September. Target close
is Q1 2027 at a valuation around €18M, subject to the diligence findings.

## Rationale

Cumulus's scheduling engine would replace two years of roadmap on the widget
telemetry side.
