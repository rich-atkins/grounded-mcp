# Salary Bands 2026

Confidential — People team and executives only.

## Engineering

- Engineer I: £45,000–£55,000
- Engineer II: £55,000–£70,000
- Senior: £70,000–£88,000
- Staff: £88,000–£105,000

## Bonus

The company bonus pool for 2026 is 8% of salary at target, paid in March.
